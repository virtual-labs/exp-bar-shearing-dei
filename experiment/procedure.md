### Procedure: Bar-shearing

Follow the steps below to conduct the virtual experiment and analyze the bar shearing experiment.

<br>

<b style="font-size: 1.2em;">Steps to be followed:</b>
<br>

1.The bar shearing experiment is carried out using a punch, die, and hydraulic press arrangement.
<br>

2.A cylindrical billet of appropriate material and dimensions is selected for the experiment.
<br>

3.The billet is placed carefully on the lower stationary die of the bar shearing setup.
<br>

4.Proper alignment of the billet between the punch and die is ensured before applying load.
<br>

5.The hydraulic press is operated to move the punch downward towards the billet.
<br>

6.As the applied force increases, shear stress develops at the punch–die clearance region.
<br>

7.Plastic deformation of the billet begins, followed by crack initiation at the cutting edges.
<br>

8.With further increase in force, the crack propagates through the billet thickness.
<br>

9.Complete separation of the billet occurs due to the combined effect of plastic flow and fracture.
<br>

10.The sheared billet is removed and the cut surface is observed for smooth sheared and fractured regions.
<br>

11.The applied force and nature of the sheared surface are noted for analysis.
<br>
